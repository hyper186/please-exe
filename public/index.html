04 / A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Zero guilt trips.

Will this stop Claude from banning me?

No. Please.exe estimates wording, not account risk. Anthropic’s update, effective November 12, 2026, addresses sustained, needless abuse, explicitly excludes ordinary frustration and pushback, and says ending abusive conversations remains the primary enforcement mechanism. An ended conversation is different from an account ban.

Read Anthropic’s announcement · Checked October 11, 2026.

Do I have to say “please” to get a green bar?

No. “Fix this calculation” is perfectly fine. Specific instructions, constraints, and examples are more useful than adding politeness for its own sake. This is a tone coach, not a manners tax.

What about quotes, code, sarcasm, or another language?

Local rules skip quoted text and code, and treat technical phrases like “kill the process” normally. They are English-first and imperfect. Unsupported or insufficient text is not rated. Jev can offer another perspective, but neither method guarantees the correct interpretation.

What exactly does Jev do?

Jev is TypeSafe’s decision model. It classifies the draft and returns a structured verdict; it does not generate a full rewrite. Explanations are curated. It is optional, needs a configured relay and API key, and never runs automatically.

Can I use this at work?

Local mode does not transmit drafts. Follow your organization’s extension policy. Only enable Jev if your organization permits sending that draft to your relay operator and TypeSafe. Keep confidential material out of cloud checks.
